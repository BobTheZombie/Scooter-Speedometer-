# Scooter Speedometer & RiderLink Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 15, 2026**

Scooter Speedometer (“the App”) includes RiderLink social features, GPS speed and navigation tools, weather information, media controls, ride recording, safety alerts, and optional notification features. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

## Information the App may handle

- **Account information:** When you create or sign in to RiderLink, Supabase Authentication may process your email address and account identifier. If you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, the provider may share basic profile information permitted by your consent.
- **Rider profile:** Your chosen rider name, avatar, scooter make/model, upgrades, friends, clubs, messages, group rides, Waves, and Ride Beacons.
- **Location:** GPS is used for speed, navigation, weather, ride recording, nearby hazards, group rides, SOS, and RiderLink. RiderLink location sharing is off until you explicitly enable LIVE, join a live group ride, send SOS, or post a temporary Ride Beacon. A Beacon expires automatically.
- **Notifications and media:** With Android permission, the App can display and optionally speak supported notifications and control active media. Notification monitoring is intended to stop when the App’s riding experience is closed or the feature is disabled. Message content is not sold.
- **Camera:** The optional backup-camera feature uses the selected device camera for an on-screen live view. The App does not upload or store camera video unless a future feature clearly asks for separate consent.
- **Ride and device data:** Speed, distance, routes, destinations, maintenance records, app settings, and diagnostics may be stored locally. Data is sent to a service only when needed for a feature you enable.
- **Third-party data:** Weather, maps, routing, hazard, ALPR-camera, and police-sighting features may contact their respective providers. Those providers may receive network information and the approximate coordinates required to answer a request.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide requested App functions, authenticate RiderLink accounts, show nearby riders and safety information, operate messaging and group rides, improve reliability, and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal information.

## Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate enabled features, with other RiderLink users according to your sharing choices, when required by law, or to protect safety and prevent abuse. RiderLink SOS does not contact 911 or emergency services.

## Retention and control

Local data can be removed by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it. You can turn LIVE location sharing off, remove temporary Beacons, sign out, and revoke Android permissions at any time. To request deletion of a RiderLink account or associated server data, use the contact information below. Some limited records may be retained when legally required or necessary to prevent abuse.

## Security and rider safety

We use reasonable safeguards, including authenticated access and database access controls. No system is completely secure. Do not operate the App in a way that distracts you while riding. Weather, maps, hazards, police sightings, ALPR reports, speed limits, and routes may be incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate.

## Children

The App and RiderLink are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as features or providers change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
